# Privacy Policy  
Last updated: November 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you use the Fasto application (the “Service”), and describes your rights regarding your data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Who We Are  
Fasto (“the Application”) is a local ordering and delivery service.  
The Service is owned and operated by **SADA AL-NAJAH DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION COMPANY**, located in Sabha, Libya (“the Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle data, you can contact us using the details at the end of this document.

---

## 2. Information We Collect  
We collect the following types of information when you use the Service:

### Contact Information  
- First name and last name  
- Phone number

### Delivery Information  
- Delivery address and location you provide so we can deliver your order  
- City / area information  
- Notes or instructions related to the order (for example: “leave at the door”)

### Order Details  
- Items you request  
- Order history  
- Order status

### Location Data  
- Your approximate or precise location, **if you allow location access on your device**.  
  We use this to help you set your delivery address faster and to deliver to the correct location.  
- The live location of the delivery driver during an active delivery.  
  This allows you to track your order in real time and know when it will arrive.

You can disable access to location services in your device settings at any time. However, some features (automatic address detection, live driver tracking) may not work without location.

### Device / Usage Data  
- Device type, operating system, and app version  
- IP address  
- Time and date of use, viewed screens, and basic diagnostics  

We use this information to keep the Service secure, improve performance, and prevent fraud.

We do **not** intentionally collect data from children under 13 years old.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information  
We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account  
- Prepare, confirm, and deliver your orders  
- Contact you about the status of an active order (for example: “your driver is arriving”)  
- Help the driver reach the correct delivery address  
- Show you real-time delivery status and estimated arrival time  
- Provide customer support and resolve problems  
- Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse of the Service  
- Comply with legal and accounting requirements where applicable

We may also keep a record of your previous orders so that you can reorder quickly.

We do **not** use your personal data for targeted advertising, ad tracking, or cross-app tracking.

---

## 4. How We Share Information  
We only share your information when it is necessary to operate the Service:

### With the delivery driver (courier):  
We may share your delivery address, phone number (if needed for drop-off or confirmation), and order details so the driver can deliver your order.

### With local sellers or stores that prepare your order:  
We may share the items you requested and any delivery notes so they can prepare the order.

### With authorities when required by law:  
We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, to protect our rights, or to prevent fraud or harm.

We do **not** sell your personal data.  
We do **not** share your personal data with unrelated marketing partners.  
We do **not** allow third-party advertisers to track you across other apps or websites.

---

## 5. Location and Tracking  
Fasto uses location data only to provide core delivery features:
- To help you set or confirm a delivery address  
- To help the driver reach you  
- To show you the live status of your active order

We do **not** request permission to track you across other apps and websites for advertising purposes.  
We do **not** run an advertising system that follows you outside this Service.

If you disable location sharing in your device settings, the app may still work, but some features (like automatic address or live driver tracking) may be limited.

## 6. Data Retention  
We keep your information for as long as:
- Your account is active,  
- We need to deliver your orders or provide support, or  
- We are required to keep certain records under applicable law (for example, for billing or dispute resolution).

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or anonymize it.

## 7. Your Rights  
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information  
- Correct inaccurate information (for example, wrong phone number or address)  
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal data, unless we must keep certain records for legal reasons

### How to request deletion of your account or data:  
Send an email to **dev@est.ly** and include:  
- Your full name  
- The phone number that you used to register in the app  

This helps us verify your identity before we process the request.

If you request deletion, we will remove your account and personal data that we are not legally required to retain.
